<precedence>

the label. Do not weigh rules against each other; stop at the first match.

2. The instrument is penal, police, carceral, or migration control →
   sécurité_intérieure_et_justice
3. The subject is a person as patient, allocataire, or dependent, or a care system →
   social_et_santé
4. The subject is an élève, étudiant, enseignant, chercheur, or formation initiale →
   education_recherche_jeunesse
5. The subject is a natural resource, an agricultural or fishing sector, a physical
   network, or the development of a territory →
   environnement_territoires_et_infrastructures
6. The subject is the organisation of the State, its agents, or an electoral or
   institutional process → institutions_et_fonction_publique
7. The content is economic, fiscal, budgetary, or labour-market →
   économie_travail_et_finances_publiques
8. Otherwise → autre
</precedence>
